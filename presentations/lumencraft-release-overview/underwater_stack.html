UNDERWATER FEATURE ARC

Underwater Became the
Signature Experience

Five release steps turned water from a surface treatment into a layered visual and audio environment.

01 · SPECTRAL

Blue-teal water

Clearer underwater scattering, controlled fog, and a more readable seabed.

02 · CAUSTICS

Animated light

Brighter moving caustics and underwater light-ray assets shaped the floor response.

03 · SUNBEAMS

Surface penetration

Forward-scattering and fog tuning introduced broad diagonal volumetric-style shafts.

04 · LIFE

Glow and dust

Bioluminescent particles and slow floating motes added depth without a particle storm.

05 · ATMOSPHERE

Sound & reflection

Original underwater ambience, chimes, and low-roughness water tuning completed the experience.
